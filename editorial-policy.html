Editorial Policy

ManthanBites aims to explain nutrition and everyday wellness topics in clear, practical language for readers in India. Content is intended for general education.

Research and sources

When an article makes factual health or nutrition claims, it should cite reliable sources such as government health agencies, recognised public-health organisations, peer-reviewed research or established clinical guidance. Sources should be relevant to the specific claim.

Accuracy and limitations

Nutrition needs vary by age, health status, activity, medicines, pregnancy and other circumstances. Articles should avoid presenting one routine, food, supplement or quantity as suitable for everyone. ManthanBites does not promise cures, guaranteed weight loss or specific health outcomes.

Review and updates

Articles should be reviewed when important evidence or guidance changes. A published or updated date should be displayed only when it reflects the actual publication or substantive update. Do not describe content as medically reviewed unless a qualified professional has actually reviewed that specific content.

Corrections

If you identify a factual error, outdated statement or broken source, contact the website owner through the contact method displayed on the live website. Corrections should be made transparently where material.

Commercial transparency

Any sponsorship, affiliate relationship, gifted product or other material commercial connection related to an article should be clearly disclosed near the relevant content.
