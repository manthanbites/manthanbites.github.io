Raat Ko Late Khana: Kya Sach Mein Problem Hai?

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 24 November 2026 | 5 min read

Seedhi baat

Khane ka koi ek "jaadui" sahi waqt nahi hota. Lekin sone se bilkul pehle bhari khana khane se kuch logon ko acidity, bhaari pet aur kharab neend ki shikayat hoti hai. Halka, thoda jaldi aur regular dinner behtar rehta hai.

Kya raat ko khane se wajan badhta hai?

Seedhi baat: nahi, sirf raat ko khane se wajan nahi badhta. Poore din ka khana, activity, neend aur stress sab milkar asar daalte hain. Lekin der raat khane ki aadat aksar zyada khane aur snacking se judi hoti hai, isliye dhyan dene layak hai.

Late dinner aur acidity

Pet bhara ho aur aap turant let jaayein, to kuch logon mein khana upar ki taraf aa sakta hai aur seene mein jalan ya khatti dakaar ho sakti hai. Isiliye aam taur par salah di jaati hai ki dinner aur sone ke beech kam se kam 2 se 3 ghante ka gap rakhein.

Agar late khana majboori hai

Shift duty, shaadi ya kaam ki wajah se der ho jaye to koi baat nahi. Bas kuch cheezein sambhal lein:

  • Khana halka rakhein: khichdi, dal-sabzi-roti, ya sabzi wala dalia. Tala-bhuna kam.
  • Portion chhota rakhein.
  • Dheere chabakar khayein, aur baad mein 10 minute halka chalein.
  • Din mein lambe gap se bachein. Warna raat ko tez bhookh lagti hai aur hum zyada kha lete hain.

Regular time kyun achha hai

Roz lagbhag ek hi waqt par dinner karne se bhookh ka pattern bhi steady hota hai. Perfect time dhoondhne se zyada zaroori hai ek aisa time chunna jo aapki routine mein roz nibh sake.

Aaj ki ek habit

Aaj dinner sone se kam se kam 2 ghante pehle khatam karne ki koshish karein.

Doctor se kab milein

Agar seene mein jalan roz ho, khana nigalne mein dikkat ho, baar-baar ulti ho, ya raat ko jaagne par majboor kar de, to doctor se milein.
