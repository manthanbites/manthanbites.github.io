Packaged Food Label Kaise Padhein? Packet Ke Peechhe Ki 7 Zaroori Baatein

Packet ke front par likhe claims dekhkar hi food choose na karein. Asli jaankari ingredients list, nutrition table aur serving size mein milti hai.

Packet ka front hamesha poori kahani nahi batata

“Multigrain”, “natural”, “high protein” ya “baked” jaise words product ko healthy dikhate hain. Lekin kisi ek claim se poore food ki nutritional quality decide nahi hoti. Isliye shopping ke waqt packet ko palatkar label padhna ek useful habit hai.

1. Ingredients list sabse pehle dekhein

Ingredients aam taur par quantity ke descending order mein listed hote hain. Jo ingredient sabse pehle hai, uski matra comparatively zyada hoti hai. Agar sugar, syrup ya refined flour shuru ke ingredients mein hain, toh product ko sirf front-label claim ke basis par healthy na maan lein.

2. Serving size samajhna zaroori hai

Nutrition table aksar per 100 g, per serving ya dono ke hisaab se information deti hai. Packet mein multiple servings ho sakti hain. Agar aap poora packet kha lete hain, toh per-serving calories, sugar aur sodium ko packet ki servings se multiply karke samjhein.

3. Added sugar ke alag naam pehchanein

Sugar, glucose syrup, fructose, dextrose, maltose, honey aur jaggery jaise ingredients bhi sweetness add karte hain. “Jaggery” ya “honey” likha hone se added sugar automatically free ya unlimited nahi ho jati. Nutrition panel mein total sugar aur, jab diya ho, added sugar dekhein.

4. Sodium ko ignore na karein

Instant noodles, sauces, namkeen, soups, packaged snacks aur ready-to-eat foods mein sodium ho sakta hai. Ek hi din mein kai packaged items khane se sodium intake badh sakta hai. Label par sodium ki quantity ko serving size ke saath padhein.

5. Fibre aur protein ko context mein dekhein

Fibre aur protein useful nutrients hain, lekin unka hona product ko automatically balanced meal nahi banata. Saath mein calories, saturated fat, sodium, added sugar aur ingredients bhi dekhein. “High protein” snack ko dal, dahi, chana, paneer ya balanced meal ka replacement samajhna zaroori nahi.

6. Front claims ko proof na samjhein

“No added sugar”, “baked”, “multigrain” ya “source of fibre” jaise claims ko poore label ke saath interpret karein. No added sugar ka matlab yeh nahi ki product mein naturally occurring sugar ya calories nahi hongi. Multigrain ka matlab whole grain hona bhi zaroori nahi.

7. Processing ko eklauta decision-maker na banayein

Ultra-processed foods par research important hai, lekin har product ka effect uski ingredients, nutrient profile, energy density, portion aur overall diet se bhi juda hota hai. 2026 ki systematic review mein kuch trials mein weight gain aur eating rate ke differences mile, lekin evidence ki certainty low thi aur nutrient profile jaise factors ko alag karna mushkil tha. Isliye ek label ya category se final judgement na karein.

ManthanBites Practical Action5-minute shopping challenge: Agli baar do similar products uthayein. Dono ka serving size, ingredients ke pehle 3 items, added sugar, sodium aur fibre compare karein. Phir apni zaroorat, budget aur portion ke hisaab se choose karein.

Indian shopping examples

  • Breakfast cereal ya muesli: whole grains, added sugar aur serving size compare karein.
  • Bread: ingredients mein whole-wheat/whole-grain flour ka position dekhein; sirf brown colour par bharosa na karein.
  • Namkeen ya instant soup: sodium per serving check karein, aur packet mein kitni servings hain yeh bhi dekhein.
  • Flavoured yogurt: protein ke saath total sugar aur serving size bhi compare karein.

Kya ek hi nutrient dekhkar product choose karna chahiye?

  • Nahi. Sirf calories, fat, sugar ya protein dekhna incomplete picture deta hai. Product ko overall diet, portion size, frequency, affordability aur personal health needs ke context mein dekhein.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Kya brown bread hamesha whole wheat hoti hai?

Nahi. Colour se whole-wheat content confirm nahi hota. Ingredients list mein whole-wheat flour ka position aur product details dekhein.

2. No added sugar ka kya matlab hai?

Iska matlab product mein manufacturing ke dauran sugar add nahi ki gayi, lekin naturally occurring sugars ho sakti hain. Nutrition panel zaroor padhein.

3. Kya har packaged food unhealthy hota hai?

Nahi. Packaged foods ki nutritional quality alag-alag hoti hai. Doodh, dahi, roasted chana ya plain oats jaise options bhi packaged mil sakte hain.

4. Label padhne ka sabse aasan rule kya hai?

Serving size, ingredients ke pehle items, added sugar aur sodium se shuru karein. Dheere-dheere baaki information bhi samajhne lagein.
