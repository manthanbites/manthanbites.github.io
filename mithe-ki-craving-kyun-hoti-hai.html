Mithe Ki Craving Kyun Hoti Hai? Bina Pabandi Ke Sambhalne Ke Tareeke

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 26 November 2026 | 6 min read

Seedhi baat

Meetha khane ka mann hona normal hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aapmein willpower ki kami hai. Bhookh, food habits, stress, sleep aur taste preference jaise factors craving ko influence kar sakte hain. Strict pabandi ke bajay balanced meals aur mindful choices par dhyan dein.

Mithe ki craving kya hoti hai?

Kabhi lunch ke baad kuch meetha khane ka mann karta hai, kabhi shaam ki chai ke saath biscuit ya mithai chahiye hoti hai. Kabhi bina bhookh ke bhi chocolate ya dessert khane ka mann hota hai.

Kisi particular food ko khane ki strong desire ko food craving kaha ja sakta hai. Yeh physical hunger se alag bhi ho sakti hai. Hunger mein kai tarah ka khana achha lag sakta hai, jabki craving mein mann aksar kisi specific taste ya food ki taraf jaata hai.

Meetha pasand karna apne aap mein problem nahi hai. Lekin agar craving aapki daily eating habits ko disturb kar rahi hai, to uske possible reasons samajhna useful ho sakta hai.

Mithe ki craving kyun hoti hai?

1. Bahut der tak bhookhe rehna

Agar aap meals ke beech bahut lamba gap rakhte hain, to tez bhookh lag sakti hai. Aise samay convenient aur tasty foods, jaise sweets ya packaged snacks, zyada tempting lag sakte hain.

2. Stress aur emotions

Kuch log stress, boredom, sadness ya tension ke samay comfort food ki taraf jaate hain. Meetha khana temporary pleasure de sakta hai, lekin emotional stress ka long-term solution nahi hai.

3. Neend poori na hona

Kam ya disturbed sleep appetite aur food choices ko affect kar sakti hai. Thakaan ke samay kuch log sugary snacks ya energy dene wale foods ki taraf zyada attract ho sakte hain.

4. Roz ki eating habit

Agar aap har lunch ya dinner ke baad dessert khate hain, to yeh routine ka hissa ban sakta hai. Kabhi-kabhi craving actual hunger ke bajay familiar habit se bhi judi hoti hai.

5. Food availability aur surroundings

Ghar ya office mein sweets aur packaged snacks aasani se available hon, ya aas-paas log baar-baar snacks offer karte hon, to unhe khane ka mann ho sakta hai.

6. Taste aur personal preference

Kuch log naturally sweet taste pasand karte hain. Festivals, family traditions aur social occasions bhi meetha khane ki desire ko influence kar sakte hain.

Kya protein aur fibre craving ko manage karne mein help karte hain?

Protein aur fibre wale foods balanced meals ka important hissa hain. Dal, chana, rajma, paneer, dahi, whole grains, fruits aur vegetables ko meals mein include karne se meal satisfying ho sakta hai.

Lekin yeh kehna sahi nahi hoga ki protein ya fibre ki kami hi har sweet craving ka reason hoti hai. Craving ke kai possible reasons ho sakte hain.

Mithe ki craving manage karne ke 7 practical tareeke

1. Apni bhookh ko samjhein

Meetha khane ka mann ho to khud se poochhein: kya mujhe sach mein bhookh lagi hai ya bas kisi specific taste ki desire ho rahi hai? Dono situations mein apni body ke signals ko samajhna useful hai.

2. Meals ko balanced banayein

Dal, chana, paneer, dahi, whole grains aur vegetables jaise foods apne meals mein include karein. Sirf chai-biscuit ya bahut chhota meal lene ke bajay aisa meal chunein jo aapko comfortably satisfied rakhe.

3. Neend ko priority dein

Roz lagbhag ek regular sleep routine banane ki koshish karein. Adults ko aam taur par 7 ya usse zyada ghante ki neend ki zaroorat hoti hai, halanki individual needs alag ho sakti hain.

4. Stress ke liye doosre options bhi rakhein

Agar aap stress ya boredom mein meetha khate hain, to kabhi short walk, music, deep breathing ya kisi se baat karna try karein. Meetha khana zaroori nahi ki har emotion ka response ho.

5. Craving par thoda pause lein

Meetha khane ka mann ho to kuch minute rukkar apni hunger aur mood notice karein. Aap chahein to 10 minute ka pause le sakte hain. Craving khatam hona zaroori nahi, lekin is pause se conscious choice karne ka mauka mil sakta hai.

6. Portion ko pehle decide karein

Agar aap mithai ya chocolate khana chahte hain, to packet se seedha khane ke bajay ek suitable portion plate mein lein. Aaram se khayein aur taste ka anand lein.

7. Har food ko forbidden na banayein

Kisi food ko completely forbidden banane se kuch logon mein uski desire aur badh sakti hai. Apni overall diet mein balance rakhein. Meetha kabhi-kabhi enjoy karna aur daily added sugar ko limit karna ek saath possible hai.

Added sugar kitni leni chahiye?

ICMR-NIN ki Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024 ke mutabik added sugar ko total daily energy ke 5% se kam rakhna chahiye. Yeh ek general dietary recommendation hai. Aapki energy needs aur health condition ke hisaab se practical amount alag ho sakta hai.

Added sugar sirf chai ya coffee mein milayi gayi cheeni tak seemit nahi hai. Packaged sweets, desserts, sweetened drinks aur kai packaged foods mein bhi added sugar ho sakti hai.

WHO free sugars ko daily energy ke 10% se kam rakhne ki salah deta hai aur 5% se kam karne ko additional health benefit ke liye suggest karta hai. Free sugars mein added sugars ke saath honey, syrups aur fruit juice mein naturally present sugars bhi shamil hote hain.

Whole fruit aur fruit juice ek jaise nahi hain. Whole fruit mein fibre hota hai, isliye juice ke bajay whole fruit ko preference dena ek practical choice ho sakti hai.

Packaged food ka label kaise padhein?

Packaged food kharidte waqt nutrition information mein total sugar aur added sugar ko dekhein, agar dono details di gayi hon. Ingredients list mein sugar, glucose syrup, fructose syrup, jaggery ya honey jaise ingredients bhi check karein.

Sirf "sugar-free" ya "no added sugar" likha hone se product automatically nutritious nahi ban jaata. Usmein calories, refined flour, fat, sodium ya doosre ingredients bhi ho sakte hain. Pura label dekhna behtar hai.

Meetha khane ka mann ho to kya options hain?

Apni preference aur health needs ke hisaab se ye options try kar sakte hain:

  • Seasonal whole fruit.
  • Plain dahi ke saath fruit pieces.
  • Thodi quantity mein homemade kheer.
  • Roasted makhana, apni pasand ke mild seasoning ke saath.
  • Chhoti portion mein apni pasand ki mithai.

In options ka matlab yeh nahi ki ye sabhi sugar-free hain. Inhe apni overall diet aur portion ke hisaab se choose karein.

Aaj ki ek chhoti habit

Agli baar meetha khane ka mann ho, to ek minute rukkar apni hunger, mood aur craving ka reason notice karein. Phir decide karein ki aapko kya aur kitna khana hai. Apne decision ko guilt se nahi, awareness se lein.

Doctor se kab salah leni chahiye?

Sirf meetha khane ki craving diabetes ka diagnosis nahi hai. Lekin agar iske saath kuch aur symptoms bhi hain, to medical advice lena important hai.

  • Bahut zyada pyaas lagna.
  • Baar-baar peshab aana.
  • Bina wajah wajan kam hona.
  • Lagatar thakaan ya kamzori.
  • Dhندhla dikhna ya baar-baar infections hona.

Aise symptoms hon to doctor se milkar blood glucose testing ke baare mein poochhein. Agar aapko diabetes hai, to sweets aur carbohydrate portions apne individual diabetes care plan ke hisaab se manage karein.

Yaad rakhein

Meetha pasand karna koi character flaw nahi hai. Apne food habits ko samjhein, regular balanced meals lein aur added sugar ko limit karne ki koshish karein. Extreme restriction ke bajay sustainable habits par focus karein.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Khana khane ke baad meetha khane ka mann kyun karta hai?

Yeh taste preference, daily routine ya dessert khane ki aadat se juda ho sakta hai. Har baar iska matlab nutritional deficiency nahi hota.

2. Kya meetha khane ki craving diabetes ka sign hai?

Sirf sweet craving diabetes ka reliable sign nahi hai. Lekin excessive thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss ya persistent fatigue jaise symptoms hon to doctor se salah lein.

3. Kya meetha bilkul band kar dena chahiye?

Har vyakti ko meetha completely band karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Overall diet mein balance rakhein aur added sugar ko limit karein. Diabetes ya doosri medical condition mein individual advice follow karein.

4. Kya fruit khane se sweet craving kam ho sakti hai?

Fruit ek naturally sweet option hai aur whole fruit se fibre bhi milta hai. Kuch logon ke liye yeh dessert ka satisfying alternative ho sakta hai.

5. Kya stress se meetha khane ka mann karta hai?

Kuch log stress ya boredom ke samay comfort foods ki taraf jaate hain. Lekin har craving stress ki wajah se nahi hoti. Apne eating pattern aur emotions ko observe karna helpful ho sakta hai.

6. Kya sugar-free products hamesha healthy hote hain?

Nahi. Sugar-free label ka matlab yeh nahi ki product automatically healthy ya low-calorie hai. Ingredients aur nutrition label dono check karein.
