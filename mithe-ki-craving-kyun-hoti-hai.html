Mithe Ki Craving Kyun Hoti Hai? Bina Pabandi Ke Sambhalne Ke Tareeke

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 24 November 2026 | 5 min read

Seedhi baat

Mithe ki craving "willpower ki kami" nahi hoti. Aksar yeh lambe gap ke baad bhookh, kam neend, stress aur meals mein protein-fibre ki kami se judi hoti hai. Pabandi se zyada regular meals aur chhoti aadatein kaam aati hain.

Craving kyun hoti hai?

  • Lambe gap: Bahut der bhookhe rehne ke baad shareer jaldi energy wali cheezein maangta hai.
  • Kam neend: Thaka hua shareer aksar meetha maangta hai.
  • Stress ya boredom: Kai baar bhookh nahi, mood khana maangta hai.
  • Aadat: Roz khane ke baad meetha khane ki aadat ban jaati hai.
  • Ghar mein hamesha rakha hua: Samne dikhne par khane ka mann zyada hota hai.

Kya try karein

  1. Regular meals: Khane ke beech bahut lambe gap na rakhein.
  2. Protein aur fibre: Dal, chana, paneer, dahi, sabzi ko meals mein jagah dein, taaki pet der tak bhara rahe.
  3. 10 minute ruk jaayein: Craving aksar kuch minute mein kam ho jaati hai. Paani piyein ya thoda chal lein.
  4. Plate mein lekar khayein: Packet se seedha nahi. Chhoti portion, dhyan se, swaad lekar.
  5. Poori tarah ban mat karein: Sakhti se aksar baad mein zyada kha lete hain.

Cheeni ke baare mein

ICMR-NIN ki 2024 guidelines added sugar kam se kam rakhne ki salah deti hain. NIN ki director ke mutabik agar cheeni leni hi hai, to roz lagbhag 30 gram tak seemit rakhna chahiye. Guidelines yeh bhi kehti hain ki "sugar-free" likhe packaged khane mein chhupi hui cheeni ya refined atta ho sakta hai, isliye label zaroor padhein.

Aaj ki ek habit

Agle meetha khane ke mann par 10 minute ruk kar paani piyein, phir tay karein.

Doctor se kab milein

Agar bahut pyaas lage, baar-baar peshab aaye, thakaan rahe ya bina wajah wajan gire, to blood sugar ki jaanch karwayein. Diabetes wale log meetha apne doctor ke bataye hisaab se hi lein.
