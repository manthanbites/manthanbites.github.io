Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

ManthanBites (manthanbites.github.io) ek educational website hai. Hum aapki privacy ka dhyan rakhte hain aur kam se kam jaankari lete hain. Yeh page batata hai ki kya hota hai.

Hum kya ikattha nahi karte

Is site par login, account ya form nahi hai. Hum aapka naam, email, phone number, health data ya BMI nahi mangte, aur na hi apne server par save karte hain.

Tools ka data (aapke device par)

Habit tracker aur 7-day challenge aapki progress sirf aapke apne browser ke local storage mein save karte hain. Yeh data aapke device par rehta hai, hamare paas nahi aata. Browser ka data clear karne par yeh hat jayega. BMI calculator aur quiz ka jawab sirf aapki screen par calculate hota hai, kahin bheja nahi jata.

WhatsApp aur call

Agar aap Free Weekly Program ke liye WhatsApp ya call se Rajni Pant ko sampark karte hain, to aap jo jaankari (naam, number, routine, goal) khud bhejte hain wo hum sirf aapki baat ka jawab dene aur program ke liye use karte hain. Hum use bechte nahi. Kripya sensitive medical details bhejna zaroori na samjhein. WhatsApp ki apni privacy policy alag se lagu hoti hai.

Bahari services

  • Hosting: Site GitHub Pages par hai. GitHub server logs mein aapka IP address jaisi technical jaankari rakh sakta hai.
  • Google Fonts: Fonts Google ke servers se load hote hain, jisse Google ko aapka IP address mil sakta hai.
  • YouTube: YouTube ke links par click karne ke baad YouTube ki apni policy lagu hoti hai.
  • Google Search Console: Hum site ki search performance dekhne ke liye iska use karte hain. Isme aapki personal pehchaan nahi hoti.

Cookies aur analytics

Hum apni taraf se advertising ya tracking cookies nahi lagate. Agar aage koi analytics ya ads tool jodein, to is page ko turant update karenge.

Bachchon ki privacy

Yeh site 18 saal se kam umar ke logon ke liye nahi hai, aur hum jaankar kisi bachche ki jaankari ikattha nahi karte.

Aapke vikalp

Aap apne browser ki settings se local storage aur cookies hata sakte hain. WhatsApp par bheji gayi jaankari hatwane ke liye humein message karein.

Badlav

Is policy mein badlav hone par upar ki "Last updated" date badal di jayegi.

Sampark

Privacy se jude sawaal ke liye WhatsApp par sampark karein. Dekhein: Health Disclaimer.
