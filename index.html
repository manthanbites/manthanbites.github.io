Mindful eating tips

  • Slow chewing: Har bite ko tab tak chabaayein jab tak khana soft aur achhi tarah break na ho jaye.
  • Bites ke beech pause: Agla niwala lene se pehle haath ya spoon plate par rakhein.
  • Screen-free meals: Khate waqt phone ya TV band rakhein, taaki bhukh aur pet bharne ke sanket samajh aayein.
  • Calm pace: Jaldbazi ke bajay sukoon se baithkar khayein.

AAPKE SAWAAL

Aksar poochhe jaane waale sawaal

Manthan Bites aur Free Weekly Program se jude shuruati sawaalon ke seedhe jawab.

Kya is program mein bhookha rehna padta hai?

Bilkul nahi. Manthan Bites kisi crash diet ya extreme fasting par aadharit nahi hai. Yahan focus pet bharne ke natural sanket ko samajhne, dheere chabaakar khane, aur bina bhukh sahe balance banane par hota hai.

Kya ghar ka normal khana kha sakte hain?

Haan, bilkul. Aapko kisi alag mehenge diet food ki zaroorat nahi hai. Ghar ki normal roti, dal, sabzi aur rozmarra ke bhojan ko hi sahi pace, dhyan aur paani ki sahi aadat ke saath khana sikhaya jata hai.

Yeh weekly guidance program free kaise hai?

Iska uddeshya community awareness aur sustainable health habits ko badhava dena hai, taaki zyada se zyada log bina confusion ke sahi lifestyle habits apna sakein. Guided weekly steps ke liye koi entry fee nahi hai.
