Mindful Eating Tips

• Slow Mastication: Har bite ko dhyan se chabaayein jab tak khana soft aur achhi tarah break na ho jaye.

• Pause Between Bites: Agla niwala lene se pehle haath ya spoon plate par rakhein.

• Screen-Free Meals: Khate waqt phone ya TV band rakhein taaki bhukh aur pet bharne ke sanket samajh aayein.

• Calm Pace: Jaldbazi ke bajay sukoon se baithkar khane ki aadat banayein.
