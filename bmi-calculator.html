Indian BMI ranges

Indians mein kam BMI par bhi metabolic risk badh sakta hai, isliye Asian-Indian cutoffs use kiye gaye hain: 18.5 se kam (underweight), 18.5 se 22.9 (healthy range), 23 se 24.9 (overweight range), 25 ya zyada (obese range).

BMI ki limits: yeh muscle, body frame, umar aur pet ki charbi (waist) ko alag se nahi dekhta. Waist ka maap bhi useful hota hai. Pregnancy mein ya 18 saal se kam umar mein yeh calculator sahi nahi hai.
