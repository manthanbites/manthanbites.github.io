EVIDENCE & TRANSPARENCY

References & Sources

Health and nutrition articles should link directly to the evidence supporting important claims. A source list should not be used as decoration: each reference must genuinely support the statement beside it.

Preferred source types

  • Government health departments and public-health agencies.
  • Recognised professional and international health organisations.
  • Peer-reviewed systematic reviews and research relevant to the claim.
  • Established clinical guidance, where applicable.

How references should appear in articles

Place links close to the statements they support. Include the organisation or author, page or study title, publication/update date when available, and a direct URL. Note limitations when evidence is mixed, preliminary or not directly applicable to all readers.

Corrections and suggestions

If a reference is broken or does not support a claim, contact the website owner using the contact details published on the live website.
