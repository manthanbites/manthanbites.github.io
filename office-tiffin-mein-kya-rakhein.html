Office Tiffin Mein Kya Rakhein? Simple Indian Ideas

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 26 November 2026 | 6 min read

Seedhi baat

Achha office tiffin complicated ya expensive hona zaroori nahi hai. Roti ya chawal ke saath dal, chana, paneer ya doosra protein source, seasonal sabzi aur zaroorat ke hisaab se fruit ya dahi rakhein. Variety aur balance par dhyan dein, perfection par nahi.

Balanced office tiffin ka simple formula

Tiffin banate waqt alag-alag food groups ko jagah dene ki koshish karein. Ek practical combination mein ye cheezein ho sakti hain:

  • Anaaj: Roti, chawal, dalia, oats ya millets.
  • Protein foods: Dal, rajma, chana, lobia, paneer, tofu, dahi, eggs ya doosre suitable options.
  • Sabzi: Seasonal aur variety wali cooked vegetables.
  • Fruit: Mausam ke hisaab se ek whole fruit.
  • Nuts and seeds: Apni zaroorat aur tolerance ke hisaab se chhoti quantity.

ICMR-NIN ki Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024 bhi alag-alag food groups se variety lene par zor deti hain. Yeh ek general planning guide hai, fixed portion prescription nahi. Aapki age, activity, appetite aur health condition ke hisaab se quantities badal sakti hain.

7 simple Indian office tiffin ideas

1. Roti, dal aur seasonal sabzi

2 roti ke saath dal, lauki, bhindi, beans ya seasonal sabzi rakhein. Zaroorat ke hisaab se kheera ya doosri fresh vegetables add kar sakte hain.

2. Vegetable khichdi aur dahi

Rice aur dal se bani khichdi mein seasonal vegetables mila sakte hain. Dahi ko alag container mein rakhein aur food safety ka dhyan dein.

3. Moong dal ya besan chilla

Chilla ke saath chutney aur ek seasonal fruit rakh sakte hain. Yeh un logon ke liye practical option hai jo subah kam samay mein tiffin banana chahte hain.

4. Rajma ya chhole rice

Rajma ya chhole ke saath chawal aur cooked vegetables ka combination bana sakte hain. Portion apni bhookh aur daily needs ke hisaab se rakhein.

5. Paneer bhurji aur roti

Paneer bhurji mein matar, capsicum ya doosri vegetables mila sakte hain. Saath mein roti aur seasonal fruit ek option ho sakta hai.

6. Vegetable dalia

Dalia mein matar, gajar, beans aur doosri vegetables add karein. Protein ke liye saath mein dal, dahi ya suitable protein food rakh sakte hain.

7. Roti roll ya homemade wrap

Roti mein paneer bhurji, cooked chana ya vegetables bhar kar roll bana sakte hain. Isse tiffin carry karna bhi aasaan ho sakta hai.

Office mein beech ke snacks kya lein?

Agar lunch aur dinner ke beech bhookh lagti hai, to apni appetite ke hisaab se simple snack rakh sakte hain.

  • Bhuna chana.
  • Makhana, kam oil mein roasted.
  • Seasonal whole fruit.
  • Thodi mungfali ya doosre nuts, agar allergy na ho.
  • Plain dahi, agar use safely chilled rakh sakte hain.
  • Homemade vegetable sandwich.

Chips, biscuits aur packaged namkeen kabhi-kabhi khana alag baat hai, lekin inhe roz ke main snack ka replacement na banayein. Packaged food lete waqt nutrition label aur ingredients list padhna useful hai.

Tiffin ko healthy banane ke saath safe kaise rakhein?

Sirf nutritious food chunna kaafi nahi hai. Office tak pahunchte-pahunchte food ki safety bhi important hoti hai, khaaskar garmi ke mausam mein.

  • Tiffin pack karne se pehle haath aur containers saaf rakhein.
  • Khane ko saaf, food-grade aur achhi tarah band hone wale container mein rakhein.
  • Dahi, paneer aur doosre jaldi kharab hone wale foods ko insulated lunch bag aur ice pack ke saath carry karein, ya office refrigerator mein rakhein.
  • Cooked food ko kai ghante normal room temperature par chhodne se bachein.
  • Agar food ko thanda rakhna zaroori hai, to use refrigerator mein rakhein; FSSAI guidance mein cold storage ke liye 5°C se neeche temperature ka zikr hai.
  • Agar food ki smell, appearance ya storage condition doubtful ho, to use khane ka risk na lein.

Khaaskar dahi, raita, paneer aur cooked meals ko office mein lambe samay tak bina cooling ke rakhna safe nahi ho sakta.

Busy office routine mein tiffin planning kaise karein?

  • Raat ko planning: Agle din ka menu pehle decide kar lein.
  • Batch preparation: Dal ya chana jaise food ko pehle se prepare karke safe refrigeration mein rakha ja sakta hai.
  • Seasonal vegetables: Jo sabzi ghar mein available hai, uska use karein.
  • Simple combinations: Har din naya complicated recipe banana zaroori nahi.
  • Lunch break: Khane ke liye aaram se samay nikalein. Dheere khayein aur bhookh-pet bharne ke signals par dhyan dein.

Office lunch ke waqt mindful eating kyun zaroori hai?

Kai log laptop ya mobile screen dekhte hue lunch karte hain. Isse khane par dhyan kam ho sakta hai. Koshish karein ki lunch break mein screen se thoda door baithkar apne meal ka taste aur appetite notice karein.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ki har meal perfect hona chahiye. Bas ek chhota change karein: lunch ke kuch minute sirf khane ke liye rakhein.

Aaj ki ek chhoti habit

Kal ke tiffin mein apne regular meal ke saath ek seasonal sabzi, fruit ya suitable protein food add karein. Jo option aapki routine mein aasaan ho, wahi chunein.

Kab doctor ya dietitian se personalised advice lein?

Agar aapko diabetes, kidney disease, high blood pressure, food allergy, pregnancy ya koi doosri medical condition hai, to tiffin ka portion aur ingredients individual needs ke hisaab se adjust karne pad sakte hain.

Kidney disease mein protein, potassium, phosphorus ya sodium ki zaroorat alag ho sakti hai. Diabetes mein meal timing aur carbohydrate portions bhi individual plan ke hisaab se hone chahiye. Isliye qualified doctor ya registered dietitian se salah lein.

Yaad rakhein

Healthy tiffin ka matlab expensive ingredients ya strict diet nahi hai. Ghar ka simple, varied aur balanced khana bhi ek achha option ho sakta hai. Apni health needs aur daily routine ko priority dein.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Office tiffin mein roz kya le jana chahiye?

Roti ya chawal, dal ya doosra protein food aur seasonal sabzi ek simple combination hai. Zaroorat ke hisaab se fruit, dahi ya nuts bhi include kar sakte hain.

2. Kya roti aur sabzi office lunch ke liye kaafi hain?

Roti aur sabzi achha base ho sakte hain, lekin meal ko aur balanced banane ke liye dal, chana, rajma, paneer, dahi ya doosra protein source add karna useful ho sakta hai.

3. Vegetarian protein ke liye tiffin mein kya rakhein?

Dal, rajma, chana, lobia, moong, paneer, tofu, dahi, nuts aur seeds vegetarian choices hain. Apni food preferences aur health needs ke hisaab se chunein.

4. Kya office tiffin mein chawal le ja sakte hain?

Haan. Chawal ko dal, rajma, chhole ya vegetables ke saath combine karke tiffin mein le ja sakte hain. Portion aapki individual energy needs ke hisaab se rakhein.

5. Kya raat ko tiffin prepare kar sakte hain?

Haan, kuch preparation raat ko ki ja sakti hai. Lekin cooked food ko safe tareeke se jaldi cool karke refrigerator mein rakhna zaroori hai. Subah pack karte waqt clean containers use karein aur office tak safe temperature maintain karein.

6. Office mein dahi kaise carry karein?

Dahi ko clean, sealed container mein insulated lunch bag aur ice pack ke saath carry karein, ya office refrigerator mein rakhein. Use kai ghante garmi mein normal temperature par na chhodein.
