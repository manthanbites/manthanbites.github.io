Balanced Nutrition • Indian Food

Roti-Dal Khate Hain, Phir Bhi Protein Kam Ho Sakta Hai?

Indian thali mein dal aur roti dono useful foods hain. Lekin poore din ka protein sirf ek meal se nahi, total food pattern aur portions se milta hai.

Protein sirf gym karne walon ke liye nahi

Protein muscles ke alawa enzymes, hormones, immunity aur body tissues ke maintenance mein role nibhata hai. Har age aur activity level ke logon ko protein ki zaroorat hoti hai, lekin exact requirement body weight, age, health aur activity par depend karti hai.

India mein dietary pattern kya batata hai?

2025 mein Nature Medicine mein publish ICMR–INDIAB survey analysis ne 18,090 Indian adults ke dietary profiles aur metabolic risk factors ka assessment kiya. Study ke group-level findings mein low-quality carbohydrates, saturated fat aur protein intake se judi patterns discuss ki gayi. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki har Indian vyakti protein deficient hai; individual diet aur needs alag hoti hain.

Roti aur dal ka combination kaisa hai?

Wheat aur pulses ke amino-acid profiles ek doosre ko complement kar sakte hain. Roti ke saath dal, chana, rajma ya lobia lena ek practical combination hai. Lekin protein ki total quantity portion size aur din bhar ke meals par depend karti hai.

Vegetarian protein ke practical sources

Dal, chana, rajma, lobia, soy chunks, tofu, milk, curd, paneer, nuts aur seeds protein provide karte hain. Inmein protein ki quantity alag hoti hai. Sirf garnish ke roop mein thode nuts ya seeds add karne se meal ka protein bahut zyada nahi badhta.

Plate ko kaise improve karein?

Agar aap lunch mein roti-sabzi khate hain, toh saath mein dal, chana, curd ya paneer/tofu jaisa protein source include kar sakte hain. Dinner mein bhi ek protein-rich component rakhein. Har meal ko complicated banane ki zaroorat nahi—ghar ke familiar foods se shuruaat karein.

Protein powder zaroori hai kya?

Most logon ke liye pehla step regular food pattern ko assess karna hai. Protein powder har kisi ke liye compulsory nahi. Kidney disease, pregnancy, chronic illness ya special dietary needs mein supplement use karne se pehle qualified doctor ya dietitian se salah lein.

ManthanBites Practical Action3-day protein awareness exercise: Teen din tak breakfast, lunch, dinner aur snacks mein khaye gaye protein sources note karein. Quantity ka rough idea likhein. Isse pata chalega ki aapke din mein protein sources consistently aa rahe hain ya nahi.

Simple Indian meal ideas

  • Breakfast: dahi ke saath besan chilla, ya milk/curd ke saath oats.
  • Lunch: roti + dal/rajma/chole + sabzi + dahi (portion apni need ke mutabik).
  • Evening snack: roasted chana, milk, curd ya unsalted nuts ka suitable portion.
  • Dinner: roti/rice ke saath dal, tofu, paneer, soy-based dish ya beans.

Protein need sabki same nahi hoti

  • Children, older adults, pregnant or breastfeeding people, athletes aur illness se recover kar rahe logon ki needs alag ho sakti hain. General internet target ko apne upar bina context apply na karein.
  • Balanced diet mein protein ke saath fibre-rich foods, vegetables, fruits, healthy fats aur adequate energy bhi important hain.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Kya dal mein enough protein hota hai?

Dal protein ka useful source hai, lekin actual amount dal ki type, cooked quantity aur recipe par depend karta hai. Apne poore din ke food pattern ko dekhein.

2. Kya roti aur dal saath khana achha hai?

Haan, yeh practical combination hai. Cereals aur pulses ke amino-acid profiles ek doosre ko complement kar sakte hain.

3. Vegetarian diet mein protein kaise badhayein?

Dal, chana, rajma, lobia, soy, tofu, paneer, milk, curd, nuts aur seeds ko meals mein rotate karein.

4. Kya protein supplement lena chahiye?

Har kisi ko supplement ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Pehle food intake aur personal needs assess karein; medical condition ho toh professional advice lein.
