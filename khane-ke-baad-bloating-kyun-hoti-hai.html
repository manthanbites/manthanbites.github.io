Khane Ke Baad Bloating Kyun Hoti Hai? Aasaan Samajh Aur Chhoti Aadatein

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 24 November 2026 | 5 min read

Seedhi baat

Zyadatar bloating tez khane, hawa nigalne, ek saath bahut zyada khane ya achanak fibre badhane se hoti hai. Dheere chabakar khana aur khane ke baad halki walk aksar madad karti hai. Lagataar ya tez dard ho to doctor se milein.

Bloating kya hai?

Khane ke baad pet bhara-bhara, tight ya phoola hua lagna, kabhi gas ke saath. Yeh bahut aam hai aur aksar khaane ke tareeke se judi hoti hai, sirf khane ke type se nahi.

Aam kaaran

  • Tez khana aur baat karte hue khana: Jaldi mein khane se hawa bhi pet mein chali jaati hai.
  • Ek saath bahut zyada khana: Bhari plate ke baad pet ko pachane mein zyada waqt lagta hai.
  • Cold drinks aur soda: Inki gas pet mein jama ho sakti hai.
  • Rajma, chhole, gobhi jaise khane: Yeh healthy hain, lekin kuch logon mein gas banate hain, khaaskar jab achanak zyada khaye jayein.
  • Achanak fibre badhana: Salad ya sabut anaaj ek din mein bahut badhane se pet ko adjust hone mein waqt lagta hai.
  • Kuch sugar-free products: Inme maltitol jaise sweeteners ho sakte hain, jo kuch logon mein gas ya loose motion kar dete hain. Label zaroor padhein.

Aasaan aadatein jo aap try kar sakte hain

  1. Har niwale ko dhyan se chabaayein, aur bite ke beech chamach plate par rakhein.
  2. Khate waqt phone aur TV band rakhein, taaki jaldbazi na ho.
  3. Plate chhoti rakhein. Zarurat ho to baad mein thoda aur le lein.
  4. Khane ke baad 10 minute halka chalein.
  5. Naya fibre wala khana (jaise rajma ya sabut daal) dheere badhayein.
  6. Ek chhoti diary mein likhein ki kaunsa khana ya kaunsi aadat pareshan karti hai.

Aaj ki ek habit

Aaj ke ek meal mein har niwale ko 20 baar chabaayein.

Doctor se kab milein

Agar bloating hafton tak lagataar rahe, tez ya badhta dard ho, ulti ho, potty mein khoon dikhe, bina wajah wajan girne lage, ya nigalne mein dikkat ho, to doctor se zaroor milein. Lactose intolerance ya celiac jaise kaaran sirf jaanch se pata chalte hain.
