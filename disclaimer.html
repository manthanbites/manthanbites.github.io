Health Disclaimer

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Zaroori baat: ManthanBites par di gayi jaankari sirf general educational purpose ke liye hai. Yeh doctor ya qualified dietitian ki salah ka vikalp nahi hai.

Medical salah nahi

Hamare articles, videos, tools (BMI calculator, quiz, habit tracker) aur 7-day challenge kisi bimari ki jaanch, ilaaj ya rokthaam ke liye nahi hain. Inhe padhne ya use karne se aapke aur ManthanBites ke beech doctor-patient jaisa koi rishta nahi banta.

Doctor se kab baat karein

Kuch badalne se pehle apne doctor ya registered dietitian se salah lein, khaaskar agar aapko:

  • Diabetes, BP, thyroid, kidney, liver ya dil ki koi bimari hai,
  • Aap pregnant hain ya breastfeeding karwa rahi hain,
  • Koi dawai le rahe hain, ya khane se allergy ya intolerance hai,
  • Eating disorder ka itihaas hai ya khane se judi chinta rehti hai,
  • Aap 18 saal se kam umar ke hain (hamare tools bachchon ke liye nahi hain).

Free Weekly Program

Free Guided Weekly Weight Management Program ek community initiative hai, jo mindful eating aur chhoti healthy habits par general guidance deta hai. Yeh koi medical treatment ya personalised diet prescription nahi hai.

Tools aur unki seemayein

BMI calculator ek simple screening number deta hai. Yeh muscle, body frame, umar, pregnancy ya pet ki charbi ko alag se nahi dekhta, isliye ise final nateeja na maanein. Quiz aur tracker sirf self-awareness ke liye hain.

Koi nateeje ka wada nahi

Har insaan ka shareer alag hota hai. Hum wajan kam hone, kisi bimari mein sudhaar ya kisi khaas nateeje ka wada nahi karte.

Emergency

Seene mein tez dard, saans lene mein takleef, behoshi, tez ya lagataar ulti, ya koi bhi gambhir lakshan ho to turant najdeeki aspatal jayein ya India mein 112 par call karein. Hamare content par intezaar na karein.

Bahari links
