Chai Peete Hain? Bas Yeh 4 Baatein Dhyan Rakhein

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 24 November 2026 | 5 min read

Seedhi baat

Chai chhodni nahi hai. ICMR-NIN ke mutabik din bhar mein caffeine 300 mg tak rakhein, aur khane se ek ghanta pehle aur baad chai-coffee se bachein, kyunki isse iron ka absorption kam ho sakta hai. Cheeni kam karein.

1. Caffeine kitna?

ICMR-NIN ki 2024 guidelines ke mutabik 150 ml chai mein lagbhag 30 se 65 mg caffeine hota hai, aur roz ka limit 300 mg tak rakhna chahiye. Dhyan rahe ki bade mug mein caffeine zyada hota hai, isliye gintee cup ke size se karein.

2. Khane ke aas-paas chai nahi

Guidelines khane se kam se kam ek ghanta pehle aur baad chai ya coffee na peene ki salah deti hain. Wajah yeh hai ki chai mein tannins hote hain jo khane se iron absorb hone mein badha daal sakte hain. Khaaskar un logon ke liye zaroori hai jinhe anaemia ya iron ki kami ka khatra ho.

3. Cheeni dheere kam karein

Din mein kai baar chai ka matlab kai baar cheeni. Ek ek karke cheeni aadhi karein, taaki swaad ko adjust hone ka waqt mile. Chai ke saath biscuit ya namkeen ki jagah bhuna chana ya fal le sakte hain.

4. Khali pet aur raat

Kuch logon ko khali pet chai se acidity ho jaati hai. Pehle paani piyein ya naashte ke saath chai lein. Raat ko der se chai neend kharab kar sakti hai, isliye shaam ke baad kam karein.

Aaj ki ek habit

Aaj khane ke turant baad chai na peeyein. Kam se kam ek ghanta ruk jaayein.

Doctor se kab milein

Pregnancy, anaemia, BP, dil ki dhadkan ki dikkat ya baar-baar acidity ho to chai ki matra apne doctor se tay karwayein.
