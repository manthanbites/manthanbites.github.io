Chai Peete Hain? Yeh 4 Baatein Dhyan Rakhein

ManthanBites team | Author | Editorial policy | Updated: 26 November 2026 | 5 min read

Seedhi baat

Chai peena zaroori nahi ki band karna pade. Lekin caffeine ki total quantity, khane ke aas-paas chai peene ka samay aur cheeni ki matra par dhyan dena samajhdari hai. ICMR-NIN ke mutabik chai aur coffee ko meals se kam-se-kam ek ghanta pehle aur baad avoid karna chahiye.

1. Chai mein caffeine kitna hota hai?

Chai mein caffeine hota hai, jo humein alert aur active mehsoos karwa sakta hai. Lekin zyada caffeine kuch logon mein bechaini, dhadkan tez mehsoos hona ya neend se judi dikkat paida kar sakta hai.

ICMR-NIN ki Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2024) ke mutabik 150 ml chai mein lagbhag 30–65 mg caffeine ho sakta hai. Guidelines caffeine ki total daily intake ko 300 mg se zyada na hone dene ki salah deti hain.

Yeh healthy adults ke liye guideline ka tolerable limit hai, roz itna caffeine lena zaroori ya recommended target nahi. Chai ke alawa coffee, cola, energy drinks aur chocolate se bhi caffeine mil sakta hai.

Cup ka size, chai ki patti aur brewing time badalne se caffeine ki quantity bhi badal sakti hai. Isliye bade mug ko ek chhota cup samajhkar count na karein.

2. Khane ke aas-paas chai peene se bachein

Chai mein tannins hote hain. Ye khaane mein maujood iron, khaaskar plant-based foods se milne wale non-heme iron, ke absorption ko kam kar sakte hain.

ICMR-NIN ki 2024 guidelines chai aur coffee ko meals se kam-se-kam ek ghanta pehle aur ek ghanta baad avoid karne ki salah deti hain.

Yeh baat un logon ke liye khaas maayne rakhti hai jinke diet mein iron kam hai ya jinhe iron deficiency/anaemia ka risk hai. Agar aapko anaemia hai ya iron supplements diye gaye hain, to chai aur supplement ke beech kitna gap rakhna hai, apne doctor ya dietitian se poochhein.

3. Chai mein cheeni dheere-dheere kam karein

Agar aap din mein kai baar chai peete hain aur har cup mein cheeni milate hain, to din bhar mein added sugar ki quantity badh sakti hai.

Cheeni kam karne ke liye ye chhote steps try karein:

  • Agar aap do chammach cheeni lete hain, to pehle use thoda kam karein.
  • Ek cup mein cheeni kam karne se shuruaat karein.
  • Swaad ke badlav ko samajhne ke liye kuch din ka samay dein.
  • Chai ke saath roz biscuit ya namkeen khane ki aadat par bhi dhyan dein.

Chai ke saath bhuna chana, thode nuts ya whole fruit jaise options le sakte hain. Lekin snack ki zaroorat aapki bhookh aur daily food intake par depend karti hai.

4. Khali pet aur raat ki chai par dhyan dein

Kuch logon ko khali pet chai peene par acidity, jalan ya pet mein discomfort mehsoos ho sakta hai. Yeh har vyakti ke saath nahi hota. Agar aapko aisi dikkat hoti hai, to chai ko breakfast ke baad ya kisi suitable time par lene ki koshish karein.

Raat ki chai mein maujood caffeine kuch logon ki neend ko prabhavit kar sakta hai. Iska asar chai ki quantity, aapki caffeine sensitivity aur bedtime par depend karta hai.

Agar chai peene ke baad neend aane mein dikkat hoti hai, to shaam ya raat ki chai kam karein aur dekhein ki aapki sleep quality mein koi farq padta hai ya nahi. Har vyakti ke liye ek hi fixed cut-off time zaroori nahi hota.

Aaj ki ek habit

Aaj lunch ya dinner ke turant baad chai na peeyein. ICMR-NIN ki salah ke mutabik meal ke baad kam-se-kam ek ghanta rukne ki aadat banayein.

Doctor ya dietitian se kab salah lein?

Agar aapko anaemia, baar-baar acidity, dil ki dhadkan se judi dikkat, high blood pressure ya neend ki lagatar problem hai, to apni chai aur caffeine intake par personalised salah lein.

Pregnancy ke dauran caffeine ke liye alag सावधानी zaroori hai. Chai ke saath coffee, chocolate aur anya caffeine sources ko bhi total intake mein count karein. Apne doctor se suitable limit confirm karein.

Chai peene se jude aam sawal

Ek cup chai mein kitna caffeine hota hai?

ICMR-NIN ke mutabik 150 ml chai mein lagbhag 30–65 mg caffeine ho sakta hai. Actual quantity chai banane ke tareeke aur cup ke size par depend karti hai.

Khana khane ke turant baad chai kyun nahi peeni chahiye?

Chai ke tannins non-heme iron ke absorption ko kam kar sakte hain. ICMR-NIN meals se kam-se-kam ek ghanta pehle aur baad chai-coffee avoid karne ki salah deta hai.

Kya raat ko chai peene se neend kharab hoti hai?

Caffeine kuch logon ki neend par asar daal sakta hai. Agar aapko raat ki chai ke baad neend mein dikkat hoti hai, to shaam ki chai kam karke dekhein.

Kya khali pet chai peena sabke liye harmful hai?

Nahi. Kuch logon ko khali pet chai se acidity ya discomfort ho sakta hai, lekin yeh sabke liye ek jaisa nahi hota.
